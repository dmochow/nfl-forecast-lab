Forecast Lab

A season-long public experiment. This page publishes the picks of a quantitative NFL model — snap-weighted Madden-rating features, regularized quantile regression — frozen before kickoff and graded against the closing spread. It exists to answer one question honestly: can a talent-based model still beat a near-efficient market? Both outcomes are informative, and the record accrues here either way.

The methodology follows Dmochowski (2023), A statistical theory of optimal decision-making in sports betting, PLOS ONE. Nothing here is betting advice.

Projected personnel — uncheck a player to see the forecast without him (weights renormalize, exactly as the model would)

Snap shares are empirical expected team-snap percentages for the player's position and depth-chart rank, estimated from every 2025 game (pipeline.snap_priors); theta weights players by these shares (renormalizing over active players). Injury chips (OUT/IR/D/Q/DTD, hover for detail and expected return) come from ESPN's live report and are shown for judgment — the model does NOT auto-remove flagged players; uncheck them yourself to see the injury-adjusted forecast. Ratings are Madden 27 overalls from the snapshot named above. A prior-season rating badge marks a player missing from the current snapshot whose last known rating was substituted. Quantile fits are independent per τ, so bands are approximate (crossings possible). This page is a sanity instrument, not a betting slip.

Built by Jacek Dmochowski. Player ratings from EA's Madden NFL (via maddenratings.com); lines and scores via ESPN/DraftKings public data; market prices via the Kalshi public API; schedules, depth charts and snap counts via nflverse. Picks are locked at the last pre-kickoff model run and never revised; completed games are graded against the closing spread, pushes excluded. For research and education only — not betting advice.
